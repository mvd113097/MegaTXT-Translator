Who knew that when mealtime arrived, Qin Lang would take hot, steaming food out of his spatial backpack—three meat dishes, two vegetable dishes, and a soup, plus fruit and dessert for after, looking as if it had just been cooked.

Qin Lang: "Want to eat together?"

Baron Edel was shocked: "You keep food in your spatial magic device?"

Qin Lang: "Yes." Although he did occasionally keep food in there, this time he hadn't. This was food Xia Xifeng had just finished making.

Xia Xifeng would cook and stuff it into the backpack, and Qin Lang would take it out immediately. Once Qin Lang finished eating, he would stuff the dirty dishes back in for Xia Xifeng to wash.

【Xia Xifeng: Can't you wash them before stuffing them back in!】

【Qin Lang: No hot water, no detergent. I'll wash them when I get back.】

The two could private message again. Along the way, Xia Xifeng chattered on endlessly, and Qin Lang occasionally responded with a single syllable to brush him off.

If it were their companions from the Dream World, they would know at a glance that Qin Lang was chatting with Xia Xifeng. Here, there were no portable long-distance communication magic devices, so Edel just thought Qin Lang was distracted.

He was very curious about what Qin Lang was thinking and racked his brains to get information out of him.

Qin Lang used single syllables to brush off Xia Xifeng while using single syllables to brush off Edel, not hiding his perfunctory attitude at all.

Baron Edel was very helpless. However, he was also relieved. Although Qin Lang didn't like to communicate, he replied politely when spoken to; he was just a normal, reclusive person, not a bad guy. *Was the danger I felt earlier really just an illusion?*

Chapter 2 (Part 3)

Just as Baron Edel was thinking this, they arrived at the monster nest.

Qin Lang scanned the red dots on the map and told Baron Edel to stay on the perimeter while he went in to take a look.

Baron Edel happened to want to see Qin Lang's skills, so he agreed. However, he requested to go with Qin Lang to scout, so they could look out for each other.

Qin Lang: "Fine, but please don't intervene, Baron."

"What 'Baron'? Too distant. Call me Uncle Edel, just like Xia does," Baron Edel said heartily. "Fine, let me see your skills."

Qin Lang didn't care about titles; he switched immediately without any hesitation.

Baron Edel's favor for Qin Lang increased a little more. *It really was an illusion before... wait... what?*

Baron Edel looked at the mountain of monster corpses in front of him, his eyes nearly popping out of his head.

*Killed them all so fast? And you killed them, so why did you stack them up?!*

Qin Lang asked: "Uncle Edel, do you need monster materials?"

Baron Edel: "Ah... yes, I do..."

Qin Lang: "How about a 50/50 split?"

Baron Edel said dazedly: "You killed them, you don't have to..."

Qin Lang: "I'll give you half. Just don't intervene; help me gather the monsters."

Baron Edel wiped his eyes: "Are you... stronger than before?"

He was, after all, a reclusive legendary swordsman; he could see at a glance that Qin Lang's strength had grown.

Qin Lang nodded: "I need combat experience."

To further observe Qin Lang, Baron Edel agreed to his request.

Soon, his mood became the same as that of Roland, the Dark Knight who had already returned to the Dark Temple.

He saw Qin Lang getting stronger the more he killed, as if he could seize power from the slaughter, like a monster born for killing.

With such terrifying slaughter, would Qin Lang's inner reason be clouded, and would he truly become a monster that only knew how to kill?

Qin Lang stopped grinding. He let Baron Edel bring his men to divide the monster corpses.

Seeing Qin Lang stuff all the monster corpses into his spatial magic device, Baron Edel was shocked again: "Just how big is your spatial magic device?"

Qin Lang: "Not very big. My spatial magic device is linked to Xia Xifeng's, so I sent them back to the farm."

Baron Edel: "???" *Is there such a magical spatial device?*

Qin Lang: "It was a gift from a god."

Baron Edel understood. *An artifact, that explains it.*

Qin Lang glanced at the sky: "That's it for today. I'm heading back, I'll come again tomorrow."

Baron Edel: "Back?"

Qin Lang gave Baron Edel a scroll: "This is a positioning scroll. Open it when you set out tomorrow, and I will teleport over. Please help me feed the Golden-Horned Horse."

After saying this, Qin Lang tore open another scroll and vanished before Baron Edel's eyes.

Baron Edel: "?" *What just happened? Where did he go? Teleportation magic? But how could teleportation magic only require a single magic scroll??*

Qin Lang returned to the farm, where Xia Xifeng was boiling a pot of monster bone soup.

Having obtained a lot of fresh monster meat today, Xia Xifeng was preparing to have hot pot.

"Go take a bath, you reek of blood, it's disgusting," Xia Xifeng said with disdain.

Qin Lang changed his clothes and helped Xia Xifeng carry the bowls.

The Light Beast and Dark Beast were practicing using chopsticks with their little claws. They weren't surprised at all by Qin Lang's sudden appearance.

Father John, who had been invited to eat hot pot, was very surprised.

He had been surprised enough during the day when he saw Xia Xifeng constantly dragging fresh monster corpses out of his spatial magic device. That could still be explained by a miracle granted by a god.

Then, Xia Xifeng suddenly shouted, "Experience enough, level up!" and buried his head in drawing a few scrolls with spatial auras. *Teleportation? Teleportation magic arrays can be activated with just a magic scroll?!*

Qin Lang used a teleportation scroll to come back for dinner and used another one the next day to go back and continue grinding monsters.

Father John's benevolent expression cracked.

Both the Light Temple and the Dark Temple had teleportation arrays. Teleportation arrays required a massive amount of magic crystals and at least two legendary mages to activate. They were only opened during the century-long Light and Dark War.

And Qin Lang—he was using teleportation scrolls to commute morning and night.

By the third day of leaving, Qin Lang even came back for lunch and a nap.

Father John had never seen such cheap teleportation scrolls.

Xia Xifeng comforted Father John, whose worldview was on the verge of collapse: "These teleportation scrolls are special; only Qin Lang and I can use them."

Father John breathed a sigh of relief: "I see."

Xia Xifeng said: "Once I level up a few more times, I'll be able to make universal teleportation scrolls. I'll teach you then! Don't be jealous, you can have them too!"

Father John: "..."

Xia Xifeng remarked: "The spatial barriers in this world are very thin, easier to teleport through than the Dream... other worlds. Although teleportation magic arrays still require a lot of energy, they should be able to launch once a day to teleport warriors and mages, right? Even slightly larger cities should be able to afford it."

*To get rich, first build roads. If this world can popularize teleportation magic arrays between important cities, why worry about it not developing?*

The Light God and Dark God, sitting high on their divine thrones, were both lost in thought.

The Dark God immediately tore through space and arrived before the Light God's throne: "What is their origin? Neither of us has portable teleportation magic that can be used by non-legendary warriors and mages."

The Light God was silent for a while, then held an index finger to his lips: "I think it is best not to ask, not to trace the source."

The Dark God asked: "Do you trust them?"

The Light God countered: "Do you?"

The two deities were silent for a long time. The Dark God spoke first: "They have not confessed, but they have not hidden it from us either. They likely come from outside the world, but they are not Transcendents."

The Light God looked toward the distant void.

As Xia Xifeng's aura strengthened and merged deeper with the world's rules, the Light God had already glimpsed a sliver of the truth.

From Xia Xifeng's aura, He glimpsed His own madness and destruction.

"I trust him," the Light God said.

The Dark God twitched the corner of His mouth: "Fine, if you trust him, I dare to as well."

He closed His eyes, His expression sorrowful.

From Qin Lang's increasingly powerful aura, He also glimpsed His own destruction.

He had once thought Qin Lang was His successor, but it seemed that was not the case.

On the farm, Xia Xifeng shouted: "Come and eat hot pot!"

The Light Beast and Dark Beast snapped out of their daze and flew toward Xia Xifeng, who hugged them and rubbed against them.

The Dark God returned to His throne.

The Light God closed His eyes.

The two deities fell into a slumber on their thrones, pouring more of their consciousness into their projections.
