Chapter 124: First Update

When Xia Xifeng opened his eyes, he found himself leaning against Qin Lang, held firmly in his arms. His entire body ached with soreness.

He let out a soft sigh; it had been a long time since he’d felt such back pain. This new body was simply not up to par.

He shifted slightly, and the person embracing him from behind stirred as well. A certain indescribable, tugging sensation from deep within his body made his expression darken.

Xia Xifeng turned his head to look at Qin Lang, whose chin was resting on his shoulder. "We already showered—why did you get back inside?"

He clearly remembered that they had separated when Qin Lang carried him off to bathe!

Qin Lang replied lazily, "It’s warm."

Xia Xifeng: "..." Believe it or not, I’ll twist you into a knot if my sleeping posture gets any worse!

Xia Xifeng tried to pull away from Qin Lang’s embrace, but was held fast, his struggles only making the sensations within his body more acute, forcing him to freeze.

It was too late, however; the soreness in his body only intensified.

Just as Qin Lang showed signs of wanting another round, Xia Xifeng pushed his palms against Qin Lang’s face. "You can do whatever you want once we get back, but I can't take any more right now!"

Qin Lang buried his face in the crook of Xia Xifeng’s neck, hiding the dissatisfaction in his eyes.

Their initial spiritual communion had been an accident, and at the time, he had thought he would never again want to be trapped in such an awkward, privacy-less state.

He didn't know when it had started, but the two of them had grown accustomed to intertwining their souls during intimacy. Although they could choose not to view each other’s memories, they could flip through them at any time if they wished. Everything the other was lay open to them.

Having grown used to that spiritual entanglement, simple physical intimacy was no longer enough to satisfy Qin Lang. Even with Xia Xifeng handing over his diary, it wasn't enough.

The more they touched, the more he realized that physical pleasure alone left him wanting; he longed to knead Xia Xifeng into his very own body.

If they were in the Dream World, he could truly merge with Xia Xifeng completely—flesh and soul entwined, without a single gap between them.

He had been able to endure the separation before they met, but once they were together, he realized his self-restraint wasn't nearly as strong as he had imagined.

Qin Lang couldn't help but wonder: in their original world, he had believed everything was under his control, that his reason was enough to suppress his obsession.

Could he really suppress it? Could he truly tolerate Xia Xifeng leaving, or tolerate him bestowing that special treatment upon someone else?

Holding Xia Xifeng in his arms, Qin Lang felt uncertain.
Perhaps he had only been self-righteous, deceiving himself all along. From the moment he first felt a sense of crisis and tried to push Xia Xifeng away, he might have already been beyond saving.
"As long as we're always together, what could you possibly not know about me? You don't need to rummage through my soul's memories to know," Xia Xifeng said, nudging the fluffy, heavy head resting against his neck, the corners of his mouth curling up uncontrollably.
Qin Lang usually acted aloof, but when he acted spoiled, he was incredibly cute.
*Don't laugh, don't laugh,* he told himself. *If I laugh, he’ll get embarrassed and angry, and he won't act spoiled like this again.* Qin Lang was just too concerned with his pride, too fond of keeping up appearances.
Qin Lang gave Xia Xifeng’s earlobe a light nip.
No matter what he was thinking, Xia Xifeng could always see right through him, making him doubt whether the gap in their intelligence was even real.
"Can we check the system update now?" Xia Xifeng urged. He hadn't looked at it since the update, and the itch of curiosity was making him restless.
"Sigh..." Qin Lang let out a soft breath, slightly dissatisfied with Xia Xifeng’s constant habit of ruining the mood. "Aren't you tired?"
"Even if I'm tired, I have to see it," Xia Xifeng replied. "I won't be able to sleep otherwise."
Qin Lang said pointedly, "I could make you fall asleep very quickly."
Xia Xifeng tugged at Qin Lang’s hair. "No more! I said no, so no!"
Regretfully, Qin Lang let go of Xia Xifeng and lay flat on the bed beside him, and together they began to study the system update.
The system’s original "Comrade-in-Arms" function had been partially unlocked. They could now share inventories, maps, experience points, and equipment, and their skills had been updated as well.
Qin Lang’s system update was simple: it merely increased his combat power, nothing worth mentioning.
Xia Xifeng, however, gained a "Self-Creation" menu. He could use materials from this world to research and craft items himself. Once a self-created item was recognized by the system, it could be "sacrificed" to unlock more skills and increase the number of slots in his system inventory.
Among the unlocked skills, aside from the survival skills that had previously been locked for Xia Xifeng—such as "Increased Running Speed," "Immunity to Poisons," and "No Fall Damage"—there was now an "Academy Panel."
The Academy Panel was a management module similar to the City of Hope panel, much like a standard simulation game. Through it, Xia Xifeng could manage the new academy he was about to build and adjust the curriculum in real-time based on the students' data.
One of the key metrics in the Academy Panel was "World Influence."
For the first time, the system had finally issued a "Main Quest."
Originally, they were required to stay until the world ended before they could return, but now, they could go back as soon as the "World Influence" progress bar was full.
Simply put, just like in the Dream World, the system still centered the tasks around Xia Xifeng, with Qin Lang serving as his assistant and bodyguard.
Xia Xifeng didn't think much of it, but Qin Lang tapped on the Academy section. "The system isn't guessing your thoughts; it’s formulating tasks based on your actions. There might be another reason for this main quest."
Xia Xifeng: "Hmm."
Qin Lang: "?"

Xia Xifeng: "?"
Qin Lang: "And then?"
Xia Xifeng: "Isn't that supposed to be your line?"
Qin Lang took a deep breath and said, "I was telling you to think."
Xia Xifeng nudged Qin Lang’s shoulder with his own. "Can't be bothered. You’ve already thought of the answer, so why should I? Can’t you put that clever brain of yours to use? Stop being such a mindless combat junkie."
Qin Lang was so amused he was almost angry. So, it’s my fault you don’t think? He just disliked following plotlines and dealing with strangers—when had he ever stopped thinking? Wasn’t he the one managing the massive mess that was the City of Hope? Xia Xifeng only had to worry about research and production!
Though, he did end up dumping everything on Damon later.
Qin Lang felt a flicker of guilt.
Seeing the sudden look of guilt on Qin Lang’s face, Xia Xifeng understood instantly and felt a flicker of guilt himself.
Ah, Damon—truly the best brother any player could have in the starter village, the white moonlight and red cinnabar mole of every player. Thumbs up!
The two exchanged a guilty glance and went back to studying the system.
Since Xia Xifeng couldn't be bothered to think, Qin Lang laid it out for him.
He speculated that Xia Xifeng’s efforts—founding the academy, promoting magitech, and leading the development of world civilization—might genuinely be affecting the world’s Creator.
When the world influence progress bar hit maximum, that would be when the hostile Creator noticed them, and the system would have to extract them. Otherwise, the system could have easily let them stir up trouble for a few more years to trigger an even greater wave.
Xia Xifeng shared his theory with Qin Lang: "Once the children grow strong enough, the parents can no longer easily dictate their fate." Qin Lang agreed with this sentiment.
The update to the mission was perhaps the system’s response to their line of thinking.
"Our goal is to max out that progress bar before the world is destroyed!" Xia Xifeng realized. "Do you have any idea when the world will end?"
Qin Lang: "A Creator cannot directly attack their own world. They must have buried a hidden danger in the underlying logic of this world, one that can be detonated at any time. In a world with gods, all rules—including the rules used to detonate the world—will inevitably manifest as a deity."
Qin Lang hadn't just been asking the Church of Darkness for theological texts to find the divine realm where Xia Xifeng might have landed.
Ever since he heard the Dragon God’s revelation about the world crisis, he had harbored this suspicion.
That Outer God, who symbolized the destruction of the world, would certainly come to the Dream World. As long as they knew the archetype of that Outer God and the time of its birth, they could deduce the time of the world’s destruction.
Qin Lang: "The Outer God symbolizing world destruction might also be a key weapon used to shatter the local rules of the Dream World once it arrives. Perhaps we can find a way to break that cycle within this world."
Xia Xifeng praised him: "Your brain works great when you actually use it, but why are you so lazy about it?"
Qin Lang: "?" We were having a perfectly good conversation—did you really have to insult me?
Qin Lang had intended to be gentle with Xia Xifeng, but Xia Xifeng simply wouldn't give him the chance.
