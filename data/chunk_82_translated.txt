### Chapter 124: Update One

When Xia Xifeng opened his eyes, he was pressed against Qin Lang’s back, held tightly in his arms, his whole body aching.

He sighed softly; it had been a long time since he’d felt this kind of lower-back soreness. This new body was just not up to the task.

He shifted slightly, and the person holding him from behind moved as well. A sudden, indescribable tugging sensation from deep within his body made his face darken.

Xia Xifeng turned his head to look at Qin Lang, whose chin was resting on his shoulder: "We already showered, why did you go back in?"

He clearly remembered that they had separated when Qin Lang carried him to the bath!

Qin Lang replied lazily: "It was warm."

Xia Xifeng: "..." *Don't tempt me to give you a good pinch for my poor sleeping posture!*

Xia Xifeng tried to pull away from Qin Lang’s embrace, but was held fast. The wiggling only made the sensation inside him more pronounced, so he quickly stopped.

Unfortunately, it was already too late; the soreness deepened.

Just as Qin Lang seemed ready to go for another round, Xia Xifeng pushed against his face with both hands: "You can do whatever you want when we get back, but I can't take it right now!"

Qin Lang buried his face in the crook of Xia Xifeng’s neck, hiding the dissatisfaction in his eyes.

At first, their souls intertwining had been an accident, and he had vowed never again to fall into such a vulnerable state of zero privacy.

He didn't know when it started, but they had become habituated to mingling their souls during intimacy. Although they could choose not to look at each other’s memories, they could flip through them at any moment if they wished. Everything the other was, was laid bare.

Having grown accustomed to this soul-merging, mere physical intimacy was no longer enough for Qin Lang. Even with Xia Xifeng giving him his journals, it wasn't enough.

The more they were physically close, the more he felt the limitation of pure physical pleasure, and the more he grew unsatisfied, wanting nothing more than to knead Xia Xifeng into his own flesh.

If they were in the Dream World, he could truly knead Xia Xifeng into his being—fused from soul to flesh, without a single gap.

When they were apart, he could endure it, but once reunited, he realized his restraint wasn't nearly as strong as he’d imagined.

Qin Lang couldn't help but wonder: in their original world, he had assumed everything was under control, that his rationality was enough to suppress his paranoia.

Could he really suppress it? Could he really tolerate Xia Xifeng leaving, or tolerate Xia Xifeng shifting his special attention to someone else?

Holding Xia Xifeng, Qin Lang was no longer certain.

Perhaps he had only been arrogant, deceiving himself. From the moment he felt a sense of crisis and tried to push Xia Xifeng away, he might have already been beyond saving.

"As long as we're always together, what could you possibly not know about me? You don't need to dig through my memories to know," Xia Xifeng said, tousling the fluffy head at his neck, the corners of his mouth curling up uncontrollably.

Qin Lang usually put on a cold, aloof front, but when he acted spoiled, he was especially cute.

*Can't laugh, mustn't laugh,* he told himself. If he laughed, Qin Lang would get embarrassed and angry, and then he’d never be unintentionally cute again. Qin Lang was just too obsessed with his image, too fond of putting on airs.

Qin Lang nipped gently at Xia Xifeng’s earlobe.

No matter what he was thinking, Xia Xifeng could always see right through him, making him doubt if the intelligence gap between them was even real.

"Can we check the system update now?" Xia Xifeng urged. He hadn't checked it since the update, and it was itching at his mind, making him extremely restless.

"Sigh..." Qin Lang let out a soft sigh, slightly annoyed that Xia Xifeng always ruined the mood. "Aren't you tired?"

Xia Xifeng replied: "I'm tired, but I still have to look. I won't be able to sleep if I don't."

Qin Lang implied meaningfully: "I can make you fall asleep very quickly."

Xia Xifeng tugged on Qin Lang’s hair: "No more! I said no more, so no more!"

With a look of regret, Qin Lang released Xia Xifeng, lying flat on his back beside him so they could examine the system update together.

The original "Comrade-in-Arms" feature had been partially unlocked. They could share inventories, maps, experience, and equipment, and their skills had also been updated.

Qin Lang’s update was simple—it merely increased his combat power, which didn't require much elaboration.

Xia Xifeng, however, gained a "Self-Creation" menu. He could use materials from this world to research and develop items. Once an item was recognized by the system, he could "sacrifice" it to unlock more skills and expand his system inventory slots.

Among the unlocked skills, besides his locked survival skills—like "Faster Sprinting," "Poison Immunity," and "No Fall Damage"—he could now unlock an "Academy Panel."

The Academy Panel was a management sector similar to the Hope City panel, much like a typical simulation game. Xia Xifeng could manage his soon-to-be-built academy from it, adjusting curricula based on the student panels.

One crucial metric in the Academy Panel was "World Influence."

Finally, the system had issued a "Main Quest."

Originally, they had to stay until the world was destroyed to return; now, they could leave as soon as the "World Influence" progress bar was full.

In short, the system functioned just like in the Dream World: Xia Xifeng remained the primary quest-taker, while Qin Lang served as the assistant and bodyguard.

Xia Xifeng didn't think much of it, but Qin Lang tapped on the Academy section: "The system doesn't guess your intentions; it formulates quests based on your actions. There might be another reason for this main quest."

Xia Xifeng: "Hmm."

Qin Lang: "?"

Xia Xifeng: "?"

Qin Lang: "And?"

Xia Xifeng: "Isn't it your turn to say 'and'?"

Qin Lang took a deep breath and said: "I was telling *you* to think."

Xia Xifeng bumped his shoulder against Qin Lang’s: "Too lazy to think. You’ve already thought of the answer, so what’s left for me to think about? Can you use that clever brain of yours for something other than brainless combat satisfaction?"

Qin Lang was so angry he laughed. *You don't want to think, and yet it's my fault?* He just didn't like following plots or dealing with strangers—when had he ever stopped thinking? Wasn't it him managing the massive undertaking of Hope City? Xia Xifeng only focused on research and production!

...Though he did dump everything onto Damon afterward.

Qin Lang felt a flicker of guilt.

Seeing Qin Lang’s sudden expression of guilt, Xia Xifeng understood in a heartbeat and felt a flicker of guilt himself.

*Ah, Damon—truly the best bro a player could have in a newbie village, the 'white moonlight and red mole' of all players—thumbs up!*

The two exchanged a guilty glance and went back to studying the system.

Since Xia Xifeng was too lazy to think, Qin Lang spoke directly.

He speculated that by establishing the academy, popularizing magitech, and leading the world's civilization, Xia Xifeng might truly be having an impact on the Creator of this world.

The moment the World Influence progress bar hit maximum, the hostile Creator would notice them, and the system would be forced to pull them out. Otherwise, the system could have let them stir up trouble for years, creating even larger waves.

Xia Xifeng shared his theory: "Once children grow strong, their parents can no longer easily control their fate." Qin Lang agreed with his logic.

The update to the quest was perhaps the system’s response to their new way of thinking.

"Our goal is to grind the progress bar full before the world is destroyed!" Xia Xifeng realized. "Do you have any idea when the world will end?"

Qin Lang: "A Creator cannot directly attack their own world. They must have planted a 'hidden danger' in the foundational logic of this world that can be triggered at any time. In a world with gods, all rules—including those used to detonate the world—eventually evolve into divine entities."

Qin Lang hadn't asked the Church of Darkness for theological books just to find the divine realm where Xia Xifeng might have landed.

After hearing the Dragon God’s revelation about the world crisis, he’d had this suspicion all along.

The Outer God symbolizing the world’s destruction would surely arrive in the Dream World. As long as they knew the archetype and birth time of that god, they could predict the time of the world's destruction.

Qin Lang: "The Outer God symbolizing world destruction might also be an important weapon for shattering the native rules of the Dream World. Perhaps we can find a way to crack it in this world."

Xia Xifeng praised him: "Your brain is so useful when you actually use it. Why do you insist on just acting without thinking otherwise?"

Qin Lang: "?" *We're having a perfectly good conversation—do you really have to insult me?*

Qin Lang originally wanted to be gentle with Xia Xifeng, but Xia Xifeng simply wouldn't give him the chance.
